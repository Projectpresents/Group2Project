Part Two

Bigger than you'd think

Gaming isn't a side hobby anymore. By most measures, it's the largest entertainment industry in the world.

$189B
Global games market revenue in 2025 — more than the film and recorded-music industries combined.
3.6B
People who play video games worldwide — over 61% of everyone online.

Source: Newzoo Global Games Market Report, 2025.

Part Three

Think Smarter, Play Better

01 - Problem Solving

Every challenge has a solution. Games like Minecraft encourage players to experiment, build, and find creative ways to overcome obstacles.

02 - Strategic Thinking

Strategy games encourage players to plan ahead, manage resources, and consider the consequences of their decisions.

03 - Quick Decisions

Fast-paced games challenge players to process information, react to changing situations, and make decisions under pressure.

04 - Teamwork

Multiplayer games teach players to communicate, coordinate their actions, and work toward a shared goal.

The real win?

Games don't automatically make us smarter, but they can give us opportunities to practice useful skills while having fun.

Part Four

The Bad Side

Addiction

Gaming too much can make it difficult to control how much time we spend playing.

Sleep

Playing late at night can interfere with healthy sleep habits.

Health

Long gaming sessions without breaks can reduce physical activity.

Responsibility

Gaming can become a problem when it interferes with schoolwork or daily responsibilities.

Part Five

Learning to Communicate

Everyday Communication
Players use English to give instructions, ask questions, and communicate with teammates.
Listening Skills
Players listen to callouts and understand spoken instructions quickly.
Vocabulary Building
Players learn new words, gaming terminology, and informal expressions through conversations.
Part Six

Real World Example

2024

The Netherlands Ministry of Defence opened ENGAGE, a gaming room where marines played games to explore teamwork, communication, leadership, and decision-making.

Source: Netherlands Ministry of Defence

Part Seven

The Transformation

1. The Beginning

Early games used simple pixels, basic colors, and limited animations.

2. A New Level of Graphics

Better hardware brought detailed environments, realistic lighting, smoother animations, and lifelike characters.

3. Gaming Today

Games like OPERATOR showcase modern tactical shooter experiences, with detailed environments and immersive gameplay.

Part Eight

Professional Gaming

1. Competitive Gaming

Professional players compete in tournaments that test their skills, teamwork, and consistency.

2. Big Tournaments

Games like Counter-Strike 2, Valorant, and League of Legends have international competitive scenes.

3. More Than Playing

Professional gaming requires regular practice, discipline, teamwork, and the ability to perform under pressure.

Part Nine

Word of the day

Immersive

Immersive (adjective): deeply absorbing, making you feel as if you are inside the experience instead of just watching it.

A: “Why do you play modern tactical shooter games so much?”

B: “It’s really immersive. The sounds and the lighting make me feel like I’m actually in the mission.”
